Grown-Up & Me · Ages 2-3

Little Kickers Enrollment

Six weeks of martial arts for your two- and three-year-old: balance, listening, first kicks, and a belt they'll sleep in.

A grown-up is on the mat for every class. It doesn't have to be Mom or Dad. A grandparent, nanny, aunt, uncle, or trusted family friend is welcome to be the one training with them. It just has to be an adult the child knows and who is responsible for them that day. Little Kickers is not a drop-off class.
