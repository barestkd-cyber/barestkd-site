# Facebook Ad Playbook — Little Kickers launch

Written 2026-08-31 for the first paid campaign: **$50, now through Sept 15**,
targeting moms in East Texas, using a Cubs hero reel.

Keep this file. The structure below is reusable for Cubs, Juniors, and testing
promos — only the audience, creative, and landing page change.

---

## The one decision people get wrong

**Do NOT use the blue Boost / Create-ad button on the post.** It locks the
campaign into engagement optimization, so Facebook buys cheap likes instead of
clicks from local parents.

**Do NOT build a totally separate ad either.** That throws away the organic
likes and comments the post is collecting.

**Do this instead:**

1. Post the copy **organically** on the page first.
2. Go to **Ads Manager → Create**.
3. At the ad level, choose **"Use Existing Post"** and pick that post.

Result: real targeting controls *and* all social proof pools on one post, so
the ad gets warmer the longer it runs.

## Campaign setup

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | **Traffic** | $50 can't feed enough conversions to optimize on signups. Buy visits. |
| Structure | 1 campaign · 1 ad set · 1 ad | Small budgets die when divided. |
| Budget | **Lifetime $50** | ≈ $3.50/day across the run. |
| Schedule | Sept 2 → end of day Sept 15 | Ends before the first class (Sept 16). |

## Audience

- **Location:** Tyler, TX **+ 25 miles** — covers Whitehouse, Flint, Bullard,
  Lindale, Chandler without paying for Dallas.
- **Age / gender:** Women, 23–44.
  *Honest caveat:* grandparents and dads pay for classes too, and the copy
  already invites them. Women-only is a deliberate narrowing, not a rule.
- **Detailed targeting:** Demographics → Parents →
  - `Parents with toddlers (01-02 years)`
  - `Parents with early school-age children (03-05 years)`
  That is as close to "moms of 2-year-olds" as Meta still allows.
- **Advantage+ audience toggle:** switch to **classic audience options** so Meta
  doesn't quietly widen the net past East Texas parents.

## Placements

Manual placements. **Keep:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Reels, Stories.
**Turn OFF: Audience Network** — where small budgets go to buy accidental clicks.

## The ad

- **Creative:** Cubs hero reel. Use the vertical cut if it exists, and make sure
  it reads **with the sound off** — most scrollers never unmute.
- **Headline:** `Parent & Me Martial Arts · Ages 2-3`
- **Button:** Learn More
- **Destination:** `barestkd.fit/mommy-and-me-martial-arts-tyler-tx`
  **Not** the checkout page. Cold traffic converts better after the program page
  sells it; that page's own button carries them to enrollment.

## What $50 should buy

In a market this size, expect roughly **40–80 real visits** and reach in the
low thousands.

Watch two numbers mid-run:

| Metric | Healthy |
|---|---|
| Cost per link click | under ~$1.25 |
| Click-through rate | above ~1.5% |

If it's badly off by **day 4**, the fix is almost always **the first 3 seconds
of the video**, not the targeting.

## Open item: the Meta pixel

The site has **no Meta pixel installed**, which is why this campaign buys clicks
instead of signups. Installing it (~10 minutes of work) would let the *next*
campaign:

- optimize on actual enrollments rather than traffic,
- retarget everyone who visited the page but didn't sign up,
- report true cost-per-signup instead of cost-per-click.

Not done — waiting on Race's go-ahead.
