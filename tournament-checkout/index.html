A full day of Taekwondo competition. Students get several chances to compete in different events and win gold, silver and bronze medals.

Open to Cubs, Juniors, Teens and Adults, white belt through black belt. We encourage every student to compete.

Testing discount: the tournament falls on our testing day, so a competing family tests another time that week at 50% off, for everyone in the family. Your discount code comes with your receipt.

Hosted by Stricklands Martial Arts.
